Installing Aester EQ

Save your session and close your DAW.

The installer places Aester EQ.component in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and Aester EQ.app in /Applications. It does not remove or migrate other products.

Apple Silicon build, ad hoc signed without Developer ID signing or notarization. If macOS blocks the first launch, right-click the app and choose Open. Restart the DAW after installation.
